Service 02 · Produced Water & Pollution Control

Environmental Impact Control & Waste Management

Produced water management is delivered as a turnkey plant covering separation, treatment, quality testing, chemical analysis, spill response, environmental risk assessment and monitoring.

  • Oil/water separation and oil recovery
  • Chlorine dosing, oxidation and electrostatic coagulation
  • Settling, solids processing and sand filtration
  • Acceptance against EGASPIN 2018 discharge limits or client reinjection specification

Service 03 · Gas Gathering, Compression & Metering

Gas Processing & Compression

Gas gathering, compressor packages, glycol dehydration and reboiling, coalescing filters, metering, knock-out drum and flare system maintenance and conversion for CNG, LPG and LNG applications.

  • Compressor packages and gas gathering
  • Glycol dehydration and reboiling
  • Coalescing filtration and gas metering
  • Knock-out drum, flare and relief system support

Service 04 · Subsea Inspection & Digital Field Data

ROV & Drone Services

ROV and drone inspection assesses subsea and topside assets in place, supporting FPSO assessments and reducing the need to tow large assets to dry dock.

  • ROV visual inspection, debris clearance and FPSO assessment
  • Flooded member detection, cathodic protection and J-tube surveys
  • BOP testing, wellhead integrity, lifting, positioning and cable laying
  • Thermal inspection, LiDAR, GIS asset registers and OGI methane surveys
