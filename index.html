EPCIC Delivery

From engineering depth to project handover.

Mantrawest's EPCIC capability covers conceptual engineering, FEED, detailed design, procurement, construction, installation, testing, pre-commissioning and handover.

  • Engineering for flowstations, flowlines, pipelines, topsides, subsea risers and gas plants.
  • OEM sourcing and procurement of critical equipment and materials.
  • Fabrication through the group yard and machine shop at Jesse, Delta State.
  • Installation, hook-up, tie-in, testing and commissioning.

Environmental impact control

Waste and produced-water management engineered into operations.

Mantrawest provides environmental impact control and waste management covering produced water, integrated pollution control and gas flare commercialisation.

  • Produced water treatment turnkey plant.
  • Offloading and debris filtration.
  • Holding tanks and oil/water separation and recovery.
  • Chlorine dosing and static mixing.
  • Oxidation and electrostatic coagulation.
  • Settling, solids processing and sand filtration.

ROV & Drone Services

Subsea inspection and digital field intelligence.

ROV and drone services support subsea inspection, drilling activities, asset assessment and digital field data collection.

  • In-place FPSO assessment and ROV visual inspection.
  • Underwater inspection and debris or obstruction clearance.
  • Flooded member detection and cathodic protection inspection.
  • J-tube, ACFM and subsea intervention support.
  • BOP testing and wellhead integrity support.
  • Thermal inspection, LiDAR, GIS asset registers and methane surveys.

EPCIC incineration

Controlled destruction of multiple waste streams.

Mantrawest's incineration capability includes rotary kiln and fixed hearth incinerators for multiple waste streams.

  • Hazardous solids, sludges, oily waste and liquids.
  • 850°C combustion criterion with two-second residence time.
  • Quench and air-pollution-control systems.
  • Stack monitoring.
  • Supply and installation.
